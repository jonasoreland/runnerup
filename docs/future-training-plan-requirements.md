# Future personalized training plan requirements

This document records the athlete's requirements for a later planning task. It does not create, schedule, import, or mark any workouts complete.

## Baseline comes first

- Schedule one running benchmark and one strength/core benchmark as separate workouts on the same local calendar date, using `Europe/Stockholm` for date interpretation.
- The athlete will complete both benchmarks and report actual results. Do not assume results or mark either workout complete.
- Generate the personalized full plan only after both results are available.

## Plan goals and structure

- Primary goals: overall fitness; conditioning for 90-minute Division 4 football; functional whole-body strength; core strength; explosive power; and resilience.
- Aim for approximately 3–4 distinct training days per week. This is not a cap of 3–4 workouts: multiple sessions may share a date.
- Let running frequency vary by goals, benchmarks, fatigue, and training phase. Do not force exactly three runs each week.
- Easy prehabilitation may be a separate preparatory session before a run or be placed after a run when appropriate.
- Include genuine whole-body strength, core progression, progressive plyometrics, and football movement skills, alongside running.
- Account conservatively for recurring hamstring, groin, and shin problems and residual shoulder weakness after dislocation. Avoid unsupported medical claims and preserve recovery.
- Use the canonical bundled exercise catalog, Swedish short step names, and supported structured fields for repetitions, duration, rest, and intensity.
- If the progressive plan is intended to start after testing, schedule no progressive training before benchmark results exist.
