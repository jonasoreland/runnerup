Relven is a free and open-source workout tracker for Android, derived from RunnerUp.

Record runs with GPS, pace, distance, heart-rate targets, intervals and spoken feedback. Review activity history and export recordings using the inherited formats. A companion Wear OS app displays workout information while the phone runs the recording session.

Training-plan import, watch heart-rate capture, strength workouts and a new interface are under development. No subscription or account is required for local recording. Optional external sync services may require their own accounts.
